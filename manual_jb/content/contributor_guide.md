## 1. Before you start

- Discuss your idea on Slack [in-core](https://ncsa-at-illinois.slack.com/archives/G9BH2ETSM) or [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/IN-CORE/discussions). Can't? Email [incore-dev@lists.illinois.edu](mailto:incore-dev@lists.illinois.edu).
- Process (GitFlow, issue first, branch naming, CLA): [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/IN-CORE/IN-CORE/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
